# Privacy Policy (draft)

> **Draft for legal review — not yet a published policy.** Plain-language
> customer-facing privacy notice for a restaurant using restaurant-agent. The
> restaurant is the data controller; restaurant-agent is its processor. Aligned
> with Kenya DPA 2019. Last updated 2026-07-12.

## What we collect

- **When you order or book:** your name and phone number, and your order/booking details.
- **When you pay by M-Pesa:** your phone number, the M-Pesa confirmation code, and the amount.
- **When you message us on WhatsApp:** your phone number and the content of your messages.

We do **not** collect your M-Pesa PIN or national ID; if you send such details,
our systems redact them before any processing by third parties.

## Why we use it

To take and fulfil your orders and bookings, process payments, send you service
messages, and — only if you agree — send you promotions. We also produce
aggregate business insights; these use summarised figures, not your personal
details.

## Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the service: our messaging provider
(Twilio), payment rails (Safaricom M-Pesa), and our hosting/database providers.
Where we use AI to generate insights, your personal identifiers (phone numbers,
codes) are removed before anything is sent to the AI provider.

## Marketing & your choice

We only send promotions if you opted in. You can **opt out at any time** by
replying STOP (or the opt-out word) to any message; we honour it immediately and
across all future campaigns.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data. On a deletion
request we remove your personal identifiers; some records (e.g. completed orders)
are retained in de-identified form where the law requires (tax).

## Security

We protect your data with access controls, encryption in transit, encryption at
rest via our providers, and minimisation of what we share with third parties.

## Contact

Contact the restaurant directly to exercise any right above, or to raise a
concern with the data protection authority.
