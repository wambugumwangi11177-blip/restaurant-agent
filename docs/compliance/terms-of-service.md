# Terms of Service (draft)

> **Draft for legal review — not yet binding terms.** Governs a restaurant
> (owner) using the restaurant-agent platform. Last updated 2026-07-12.

## 1. The service

restaurant-agent provides point-of-sale, inventory, reservations, WhatsApp
messaging, M-Pesa payment handling, and AI-assisted business insights. AI outputs
are **advisory**; the owner is responsible for decisions and for any message sent
to customers. Actions that change data or money (e.g. price approvals, marketing
sends) require the owner's explicit action.

## 2. Accounts & access

You are responsible for your account credentials and for the roles you grant
staff. We provide role-based access control and optional multi-factor
authentication; enabling them is your choice.

## 3. Your responsibilities as data controller

For customer personal data you process through the platform, you are the data
controller. You must have a lawful basis (e.g. consent for marketing), provide a
privacy notice, and honour data-subject requests. The platform gives you the
tools (consent capture, opt-out, erasure) to do so.

## 4. Payments

M-Pesa transactions are handled via Safaricom Daraja. The platform records and
reconciles payments but is not the payment processor and is not liable for the
underlying payment rails.

## 5. Subscription & billing

Access may be provided under a subscription plan (free/pro/enterprise). Plan state
is recorded per tenant. Payment terms for paid plans are set out separately.

## 6. Acceptable use

No unlawful, fraudulent, or abusive use; no sending unsolicited marketing to
customers who have not consented or who have opted out.

## 7. Availability & liability

The service is provided on a commercially reasonable-effort basis. See the
operations and reliability documentation for availability targets. Liability is
limited as set out in the signed commercial agreement.

## 8. Data protection

Our handling of personal data is described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md)
and [RoPA](ropa.md). A data processing agreement governs our role as your processor.

## 9. Termination & data

On termination you may export your data; after a defined window, data is deleted
per the retention policy, subject to legal retention obligations.
