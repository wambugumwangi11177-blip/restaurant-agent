# P7 — Staff entry screens: a design note

**Status: outline only, not built.** SPEC §4 says to write this from what P1–P6 taught and stop. Nothing here is code; every claim about what exists was checked against the repository on 18 Oct 2026, and the confidence tags say how sure I am of the rest: **[Certain]** has evidence in the repo or in this build's measurements, **[Likely]** is a strong inference, **[Guessing]** fills a gap and should be tested with a real storekeeper before it is built.

## 1. Why these screens, and why now

The owner's Home is only as good as what is entered behind it. Counting the blocks the build marked `as_entered` or `learning`, six engines wait on a person typing or counting something:

| What is entered | Who | Engine that reads it | What the owner loses when it is late |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock counts, and what the store hands the kitchen | storekeeper | missing stock (`ai/stock_custody`), "Missing stock" on Home | the leak the demo plants (the kitchen drawing a third more beef than sales explain) is invisible; with stale counts the number is a guess |
| Deliveries received (quantity actually in) | storekeeper | supplier reliability, short-delivery signal (P13) | "Kenchic delivers 56 of 60" cannot be seen; a supplier with one delivery must not read 100% reliable (D3) |
| Cash drawer count at shift end | manager | cash count check, Tuesday-night pattern (P7) | the first screen's "did the cash balance?" says nothing |
| Expenses and bills paid | manager | P&L, costs covered, bill radar, cost spikes | "kept" is wrong by whatever is missing; the P&L says which lines are not entered, but cannot fill them |
| Waste | storekeeper / kitchen | missing stock (waste is counted as use on purpose) | a written-off box of tilapia looks like theft |
| The M-Pesa statement (S6.1) | manager / accountant | the M-Pesa check | the second money check is off |

**[Certain]** All six already have a backend: `POST /stock/transfers` (+`/confirm`, `/request`, `/fulfil`), `POST /stock/counts`, `POST /purchase-orders/{id}/receive`, `POST /inventory/{id}/adjust`, `POST /cash-reconciliation/counts`, `POST /owner/money/expenses` (+`/bills/{id}/pay`), `POST /owner/mpesa/statement`. **[Certain]** The tier pages exist under `app/staff/{manager,stockkeeper,controller,kitchen,supervisor,waiter}` but they are the old dashboards cut down by role (SPEC §2: "`/dashboard` stays for managers and generic tenants until the staff dashboards are designed (P7)"); none is built around a one-minute entry.

## 2. What P1–P6 taught that decides the design

1. **Every figure carries its state, and entry is where that state is earned.** An `as_entered` block says "up to 14 Oct"; a restaurant whose storekeeper counts weekly shows "counted 6 days ago" next to every stock figure. The screen should say, on completion, what the owner will now see differently ("Beef is now 40.0 kg; Home's missing-stock figure is up to date as of now"). *Evidence: owner/states.py, S4.x.*
2. **A repeated entry must be harmless.** The statement importer is idempotent on M-Pesa's receipt number; the OS chat is idempotent on `client_message_id`; both exist because a retry on a bad connection happened or was foreseen. Every entry call needs a client-generated key, and the server must treat a repeat as the same entry. **[Certain]** `POST /stock/counts` and `/cash-reconciliation/counts` have no such key today.
3. **Never delete; void.** The money ledger voids an expense (who, when, why) and an upload can be removed; nothing the owner's numbers rest on disappears silently. Staff screens get "Undo for ten minutes, then ask the owner", not an edit-in-place.
4. **The owner's quiet hours and daily limit do not apply to staff** (S5.3). A storekeeper must hear "Beef is running out" at 23:00. Entry screens therefore need their own notion of "needs a response now" that is not the owner's.
5. **Cash and fraud flags go to the owner alone** (S5.3), and the person whose count is short is possibly the one entering it. The count screen must not show the expected amount before the count is entered (a blind count), or the variance is a number to match, not to find. **[Likely]** Today `POST /cash-reconciliation/counts` takes the counted amount with the window start/end; whether the form that calls it shows the expected total is a front-end question to check first.
6. **Large things are small until they are not.** The 1 MB request limit nearly stopped statements; a staff screen that attaches a receipt photo (the expense model already has `receipt_ref`, "an opaque storage key") needs the same deliberate, bounded allowance, or client-side resizing to under 300 KB. **[Guessing]** that phones resize before upload; test on a cheap Android.
7. **Time-of-day and date flakiness is a design input, not a test problem.** Three of this build's bugs (the Friday window edge, the Sunday electricity bill, the owner reminder in the night) came from behaviour that changes with the hour or day. Entry screens inherit it: "today's count" means a Nairobi day, a shift that crosses midnight belongs to the day it started, and anything dated must say the date it means.
8. **The row-level honesty rule.** The statement parser lists what it did not read, with row numbers. The entry screens' equivalent: a field the person left alone is shown as "left blank", never as zero.

## 3. The three roles, one screen each

Constraints for all: phone first (390 px is the E6 width), one thumb, pick from lists, no free text where a list will do, numbers on a keypad in whole units with a stepper for halves, every control at least 40 px tall (an E6 check that exists today), Nairobi time by default and never asked, the person's name never asked (they are signed in).

### 3.1 Storekeeper (target: under one minute per entry)

| Entry | Taps | Fields | Defaults and guards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Receive a delivery** | pick the open order (the next one expected is first) → for each line, type what actually came (default = ordered) → Done | quantity received per line; "short" is not a button, it is a smaller number | shows the shortfall in words before saving ("4 kg short of 60, KES 3,200"); a received quantity above the ordered one asks "More than ordered, right?" once |
| **Issue to the kitchen** | pick the item (the ones the kitchen asked for first) → quantity → Done | item, quantity, "for" (kitchen is the default) | the unit is shown beside the keypad; a quantity above the store's balance is refused with the balance |
| **Count** | pick a shelf group → for each item, the keypad (a blind count: the book figure is hidden until the group is saved) → Done | counted quantity | skip is allowed and recorded as "not counted", never zero; the group's variance appears after saving, to the storekeeper as "check these 2", not as a verdict |
| **Waste** | item → quantity → reason from five (spoiled, dropped, returned, staff meal, other) | | the reason is a list because the engine reads it ("waste" is counted as use on purpose; PROGRESS S5.2, finding 3) |

**[Likely]** Receiving and issuing are the two daily entries; counting is weekly; waste happens in bursts. The home screen of this role is therefore a list of what is *waiting* (an expected delivery, a request from the kitchen), not a menu of forms.

### 3.2 Manager

| Entry | Taps | Fields | Defaults and guards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Close the shift** | the drawer count (blind) → card/M-Pesa totals if the till does not give them → Done | counted cash, window start/end defaulted from the shift | tolerance is the owner's setting; the manager is told "balanced" or "short — the owner has been told", never the amount before counting |
| **Add an expense** | category (the Kenyan P&L lines, 19 standard) → amount → paid by (cash / M-Pesa / bank) → Done | category, amount, method, supplier (optional, from a list of past ones), photo (optional) | today's date; "this looks like a repeat of the one at 10:12" if the same category and amount were entered an hour ago |
| **Pay a bill** | the bills due this week, tap one → confirm the amount | | uses the existing `/bills/{id}/pay`, which records the expense and links it |

### 3.3 Accountant (controller)

Not an entry screen: a **read and export** screen. The monthly P&L with the month picker, the Excel and PDF export (`/owner/money/pnl/export`, built in S3.4), the "what is not entered" list from the P&L (`missing_categories`), and the M-Pesa statement upload (S6.1) with its "not read" list. **[Certain]** all of it exists as endpoints; what is missing is a page that is not the owner's Home. The one new thing worth building here is a *close the month* checklist that turns "not entered" into a to-do list the manager can see.

## 4. What would make it fail

- **Entry that takes longer than the work it records.** The target of one minute is a promise to test, not a number to hope for: an E6 test per entry that counts taps and fails above, say, eight. **[Guessing]** eight; measure with two real storekeepers first.
- **Connectivity.** **[Likely]** A kitchen store has patchy signal. Entries should save on the phone first ("saved here, sending") and carry their idempotency key; the owner's Home should say when entries are waiting to arrive rather than show a stale number as fresh.
- **Role creep.** The staff-roles skill and `lib/permissions.ts` define who may do what; every new entry endpoint needs the tenant-isolation test the spec requires (§0.3) *and* a role test per tier, as the money and statement endpoints have.
- **The owner reading staff mistakes as theft.** Every flag the owner sees from these entries says "a reason to look", not a verdict (§0.3 "signals, not verdicts"); the staff screen must use the same words, because staff will read them too.

## 5. Suggested order, if it is built

1. **Idempotency keys and a blind count** on the two existing count endpoints (small, backend, unblocks everything). 
2. **Storekeeper: receive and issue** (the two daily entries; feed supplier reliability and missing stock).
3. **Manager: close the shift and add an expense** (feed the cash check and the P&L).
4. **Accountant page** (read and export, nothing new in the backend).
5. **Count and waste screens** (weekly and bursty; the engines already cope with stale counts, they just say so).

Each step is a sprint under SPEC §0.2: tests and an E6 task first (at 390 px, with a tap budget), gate, evidence in PROGRESS, one commit.

## 6. Questions for the owner (the build could not answer these)

1. Does the storekeeper have a phone of their own, or does the shop share one? (Decides sign-in, and whether a "who is this?" step is needed.)
2. Who counts the drawer: the manager alone, or two people? (Decides whether the second person's name is asked.)
3. Is the kitchen allowed to request stock from the phone, or only on paper today? (The `transfers/request` endpoint exists.)
4. What is already written in a book that the screens should *replace* rather than add to?
